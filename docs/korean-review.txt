14. field_unreadable (reviewed, noun phrase)

15. continuity_break (reviewed, noun phrase)

16. no_balance_anchor (reviewed, noun phrase)

18. possible_intraday_peak (reviewed, noun phrase)

19. currency_conflict (reviewed, noun phrase)

20. currency_unknown (reviewed, noun phrase)

21. account_number_masked (reviewed, noun phrase)

25. ownership_unknown (reviewed, noun phrase)

27. page_not_read (reviewed, noun phrase)

# Indicative Form 8938 preparer panel

The collapsed English panel is an indicative comparison of the uploaded accounts, for preparer review. Other specified foreign financial assets and spouse-only accounts are not inventoried; client eligibility and return exceptions are not assessed. It does not determine whether anyone files. FBAR and Form 8938 use distinct calculations, and the Form 8938 engine has not been independently validated.

The intake JSON may state `household.filing_status` (`single`, `mfj`, `mfs`, `hoh`, `qss` or null), `household.lives_abroad` (whether the qualifying foreign residence test is met, true/false/null), and `household.spouse_specified_individual` (true/false/null). Each account can state `ownership`, `financial_interest` and `joint_with_spouse`. Missing answers stay unknown. Known fields have strict types; extra intake metadata is retained but does not drive calculations. The source and all stated/effective facts are in the signed worksheet.

The preparer determines the household facts. The panel is read-only for household inputs; supply corrections in the intake before signing. Account ownership and financial interest can each be corrected with an audit note. A correction changes only its named fact. If sole or joint ownership is stated alongside no financial interest, the account is unresolved for this comparison until reviewed; signature authority and financial interest may coexist. Preparer-only classification review items require acknowledgment but do not enter the Korean client summary.

Year-end and any-time bounds are calculated separately using unrounded USD Decimals and the recorded tax-year-end exchange rate. The any-time lower bound is the greater of the year-end lower sum and the largest single-account lower bound; the upper is the sum of individual upper bounds when all are known. This is a bound, not an exact simultaneous daily aggregate. The display rounds outward to cents; additional digits are retained when rounding could obscure a threshold crossing. Exact calculation values remain in the signed JSON export.

The source lists include candidate statement evidence, including closing readings, conflicting readings, and transaction lines involved in possible annual bounds. A missing upper bound stays unknown, and a finite one does not certify that all assets have been provided. Per-account classifications and their intake/correction source notes remain visible. Changes to an account's evidence or shared household context reopen applicable acknowledgments; changes to any signed worksheet data invalidate the sign-off on restart.
